# Password Reset Issues

## Common Symptoms
- Forgotten company account password.
- Account locked after too many failed attempts.
- Password reset email never arrives.

## Common Causes
- Too many incorrect login attempts triggering an account lockout policy.
- Reset email delivered to spam/junk folder.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) device lost or unreachable.
- Password does not meet the company's complexity policy.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
1. Go to the company self-service portal (e.g. portal.company.com/reset) and choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter your registered company email address and complete the identity check (usually MFA code or security questions).
3. Check the spam/junk folder for the reset email if it does not arrive within 5 minutes.
4. When setting a new password, ensure it meets policy: minimum 12 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol, and is not one of your last 5 passwords.
5. If the account shows as "locked", wait 15 minutes for an automatic unlock, or contact IT for a manual unlock.
6. If you no longer have access to your MFA device, this cannot be self-served for security reasons - a support ticket with identity verification is required.

## When to Escalate
Escalate immediately (do not just create a normal ticket) if the user suspects their account was compromised by someone else, or if MFA access is permanently lost, since this is security-sensitive and needs verified human handling.
